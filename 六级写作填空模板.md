# 六级写作 · 填空式模板

> 用法：把【　】里的空填上，就是一篇完整作文。
> 括号里是提示，不用抄。三套框架按题目类型选一套，别混用。

---

## 模板 A｜现象解释型
**适用题目**：近年出现某种现象 / 某现象越来越普遍 / 分析原因

**第一段（描述现象 + 引出话题，约 50 词）**

> In recent years, 【现象，如 the increasing number of college students who...】 has aroused widespread concern in our society. According to a recent survey, 【数据或补充说明，如 nearly 60% of students...】. This phenomenon has drawn attention from both 【群体一，如 educators】 and 【群体二，如 parents】.

**第二段（分析原因，2–3 条，约 90 词）**

> Several factors contribute to this phenomenon. To begin with, 【原因一，如 the fierce competition in the job market】. What's more, 【原因二，如 the easy access to information on the Internet】. In addition, 【原因三，如 the lack of guidance from schools】. Take 【具体例子】 as an example: 【一句话说明例子】.

**第三段（评价 + 建议，约 50 词）**

> In my view, this trend is 【评价，如 understandable but not entirely desirable】. On the one hand, 【好处】. On the other hand, 【隐患】. Therefore, it is high time that we 【建议动作，如 attached more importance to...】.

## 模板 B｜观点论证型
**适用题目**：有人认为A，有人认为B / 你是否同意某观点 / 讨论某做法的利弊

**第一段（引出争议 + 表明立场，约 55 词）**

> When it comes to 【话题，如 whether students should take part-time jobs】, opinions vary from person to person. Some people hold that 【一方观点】, while others argue that 【另一方观点】. As far as I am concerned, I am in favor of 【你的立场】.

**第二段（两条理由 + 例子，约 100 词）**

> My view is supported by two main reasons. First of all, 【理由一】. This can be illustrated by 【例子】. Second, 【理由二】. For instance, 【例子】. Admittedly, 【让步，承认对方有一定道理，如 it is true that...】. However, 【转折，说明为什么你的立场更站得住】.

**第三段（总结 + 升华，约 45 词）**

> Taking all these factors into consideration, we may safely conclude that 【重申立场】. Only in this way can we 【正面结果，如 make the most of our college life】.

## 模板 C｜问题解决型
**适用题目**：某问题日益严重 / 如何解决某问题

**第一段（提出问题 + 说明严重性，约 55 词）**

> Nowadays, 【问题，如 the problem of Internet addiction among teenagers】 has become increasingly serious. It not only 【危害一】, but also 【危害二】. If left unchecked, it may 【后果】.

**第二段（解决方案，2–3 条，约 95 词）**

> To solve this problem, several measures should be taken. First, 【措施一，主语最好是政府/学校/个人】 should 【动作】. Second, 【措施二】. Last but not least, 【措施三】. Only when 【条件】 can this problem be effectively addressed.

**第三段（展望，约 40 词）**

> In conclusion, 【问题】 is not something we can ignore. With the joint efforts of 【各方】, I am confident that 【积极结果】.

## 完整范文（模板 B 填好的样子）

**题目：Should College Students Take Part-time Jobs?**

> When it comes to whether college students should take part-time jobs, opinions vary from person to person. Some people hold that part-time jobs distract students from their studies, while others argue that such experience is valuable for their future careers. As far as I am concerned, I am in favor of taking part-time jobs, provided that students keep a proper balance.
>
> My view is supported by two main reasons. First of all, part-time jobs offer students a chance to apply what they have learned in class to real situations. This can be illustrated by a student majoring in marketing who works as a sales assistant during weekends and gradually understands how consumers actually behave. Second, working part-time helps students develop communication skills and a sense of responsibility, which can hardly be obtained from textbooks. For instance, a student who serves customers must learn to deal with complaints patiently. Admittedly, it is true that working too many hours may affect academic performance. However, as long as the time is well arranged, the benefits clearly outweigh the drawbacks.
>
> Taking all these factors into consideration, we may safely conclude that part-time jobs are beneficial to college students. Only in this way can we make the most of our college life and prepare ourselves for the challenges ahead.

## 精听三步（每天 20 分钟，比看剧有效十倍）

1. **盲听一遍**：不看文本，只抓大意，听完用一句话说出讲了什么。
2. **逐句听写**：一句一句暂停，写下来。写不出的地方标记，反复听三遍再放弃。
3. **对答案**：把听不出的词单独抄出来，朗读十遍，第二天再听同一段。

材料只用**真题听力**，不要用影视剧。

---

## 作文时间分配（考场 30 分钟）

- 审题 + 列提纲：3 分钟
- 写第一段：5 分钟
- 写第二段：12 分钟
- 写第三段：5 分钟
- 检查：5 分钟（重点查时态、单复数、拼写）

> 检查比多写一句更有用。语法错一个扣的不是一分，是印象分。
